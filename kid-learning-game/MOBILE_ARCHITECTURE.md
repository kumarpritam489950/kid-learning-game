## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to rewrite my code?**  
A: No! Your HTML/CSS/JS works as-is. Capacitor just wraps it.

**Q: Can I update the app after publishing?**  
A: Yes! Just update your files, rebuild, and upload new version.

**Q: Will it work offline?**  
A: Yes! All assets are bundled with the app. No internet needed.

**Q: What about data sync across devices?**  
A: Currently local-only. Can add Firebase/backend for cloud sync.

**Q: Can I monetize the app?**  
A: Yes! Add Google AdMob or in-app purchases via Capacitor plugins.

**Q: How to add more native features?**  
A: Install Capacitor plugins (camera, geolocation, etc.) via npm.
